Glitchless

The classic Ecclesia run

This category uses none of the newly found major glitches to beat the game as a mostly intended way.

Don't think it'll be easy though! Because you can't level up from boss rush you have to some very difficult boss fights in this category and you will die quickly!

Introduction

This will not be a room by room guide, instead I will provide a sample section for each boss and level with optimal movement.

This category has somewhat complicated routing, as there are points where depending on if you get a Magical Ticket drop in the first few areas you may have to do things in a different order in the mid game.

Because of this make sure to open Brown Chests for a Magical Ticket in Monastary, Ruvas Forest, and Minera Prison Island

For further information regarding the first two rooms of the game, visit the tutorial section
