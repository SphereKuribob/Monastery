Prelude - The Glitchless Era

For many years, Ecclesia didn't really have many glitches or sequence breaks, so the Any% category was basically just the intended route of the game

The category showed off the fundamentals of the game from Magnes Dashing and Union Cancels to unique movement techniques with Redire

A legenary runner, Hetfield90, would grind this category all the way down to a 48:59 on September 8, 2019, where the record would stay for a while

A great run with very few mistakes, which wouldn't be beaten until liwzero's run after the Dominus Collection's release

The glitchless category's record is currently held by apple_for_you, with a time of 47:41, making use of newer strategies, which I'll discuss in a future glitchless guide

Many runners thought that Ecclesia was unbreakable, but once the Dominus Collection released, a single disocvery would change Any% forever

The Magical Ticket Glitch

Shortly after the release of the collection, halgorithm discovered the Magical Ticket Glitch, allowing us to use the Magical Ticket anywhere

The Magical Ticket allowed runners to not only skip many backtracking segments, but it allowed them to save during boss rush mode with ease, which would instantly make Shanoa Level 50

Before this, Volaticus Clipping was the only way to achieve a similar effect, which was useless to the prior route due to being so late into the game

LonelyRTA would take advantage of these new strategies, completing a run that showcased the potential of the glich

While it was still behind record, the run was an early demonstration of just how much time this new discovery could save, and soon it would lead to an even faster run
